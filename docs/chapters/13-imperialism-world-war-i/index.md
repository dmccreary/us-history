# U.S. Imperialism and World War I (1898–1920)

## Summary

This chapter covers the Spanish-American War, U.S. acquisition of overseas territories, the Panama Canal, dollar diplomacy, and Woodrow Wilson's progressive foreign policy — then shifts to U.S. entry into World War I, home front mobilization, wartime propaganda (the Committee on Public Information), the Fourteen Points, and the failed Treaty of Versailles ratification. The chapter develops the analytical skills of propaganda analysis and historical myth identification.
