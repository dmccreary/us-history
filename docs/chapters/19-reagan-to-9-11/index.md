# From Reagan to 9/11 (1975–2001)

## Summary

This chapter spans the Ford and Carter years of stagflation and the Iranian Hostage Crisis through the Reagan Revolution, supply-side economics, the fall of the Berlin Wall, the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and the Gulf War — ending with 9/11 as the pivot to a new era. The chapter synthesizes Political Party Evolution (from New Deal coalition through Reagan realignment), the history of Third Party Movements, and Lobbying and Interest Groups as cross-era institutional concepts.
