# Contemporary America and the Digital Age (2001–Present)

## Summary

This chapter examines the post-9/11 world through the War on Terror, Iraq War, Hurricane Katrina, the 2008 financial crisis, the Obama presidency and Affordable Care Act, the rise of social media and political polarization, Black Lives Matter, and the Trump and Biden presidencies. It synthesizes the history of Income Inequality, Wealth Concentration, Climate Change Policy, Media and Public Opinion, Investigative Journalism, and Freedom of the Press as cross-era themes that culminate in the present moment.
