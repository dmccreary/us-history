# The Age of AI and Technology Power (2010–Present)

## Summary

This capstone chapter traces the historical arc of artificial intelligence — from early machine learning through the deep learning revolution — as a geopolitical force reshaping national security, warfare, and democracy. Students examine the semiconductor supply chain's fragility (U.S.–China chip wars, TSMC, ASML, NVIDIA), AI-enabled disinformation and election interference, state-sponsored cyber warfare (Stuxnet, SolarWinds, Volt Typhoon), drone warfare in Ukraine as a live AI laboratory, and autonomous weapons systems. The AP theme of Work, Exchange, and Technology provides the capstone thematic synthesis.
