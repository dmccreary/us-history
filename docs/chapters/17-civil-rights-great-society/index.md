# Civil Rights and the Great Society (1954–1968)

## Summary

This chapter covers the Civil Rights Movement from Brown v. Board of Education through sit-ins, Freedom Riders, Birmingham, the March on Washington, and the Civil Rights and Voting Rights Acts — alongside the Great Society programs (Medicare, Medicaid, Immigration Act of 1965) that constituted the most ambitious domestic legislation since the New Deal. Cross-era concepts including the Social Safety Net's evolution, immigration policy history, and the full arc of desegregation history are synthesized here.
