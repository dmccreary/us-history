# The Early Cold War (1945–1960)

## Summary

This chapter traces the origins of the Cold War from the Yalta Conference and Berlin Blockade through containment doctrine, the Marshall Plan, NATO, the Korean War, McCarthyism, and the nuclear arms and space races with the Soviet Union. The AP theme of America in the World is introduced here, and Arms Race Dynamics as a systems thinking concept illuminates the self-reinforcing logic of nuclear escalation.
