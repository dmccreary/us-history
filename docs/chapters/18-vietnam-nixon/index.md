# Vietnam, Nixon, and Social Movements (1965–1975)

## Summary

This chapter examines the escalation of Vietnam from the Gulf of Tonkin Resolution through the Tet Offensive, the anti-war movement, Kent State, and the Pentagon Papers — alongside Nixon's détente with China, and his downfall in Watergate. The chapter also covers the era's sweeping social movements: second-wave feminism, Roe v. Wade, the American Indian Movement, and César Chávez's farmworkers movement. The AP theme of America and Culture is synthesized here.
