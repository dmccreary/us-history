# World War II and the Home Front (1939–1945)

## Summary

This chapter examines U.S. involvement in World War II from the Lend-Lease Act and Pearl Harbor through D-Day, island-hopping, and the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Equally important is the home front: women entering the workforce, war production mobilization, Japanese American internment (Korematsu v. United States), and the Holocaust's challenge to U.S. neutrality.
