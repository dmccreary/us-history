HISTORICAL ACCURACY NOTES:
- The original Mayflower Compact document no longer exists (it was lost); what survives
  is a transcription in William Bradford's journal "Of Plymouth Plantation" — the document
  here represents a plausible period recreation, not a claim to show the original
- The compact was not a democratic constitution in the modern sense — it was a practical
  agreement to establish "just and equal laws" among the colonists, primarily to prevent
  the "Strangers" (non-Separatists) from defecting once they landed outside Virginia Company
  jurisdiction — this nuance can be noted in a caption
- 41 of the 102 passengers signed; women and servants did not sign
