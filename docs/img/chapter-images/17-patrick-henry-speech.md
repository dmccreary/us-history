The scene takes place inside St. John's Church, Richmond, Virginia — a simple, modest
Anglican church built in 1741, used because no building in Richmond was large enough to
hold the delegates. The viewpoint is from slightly above and behind the delegates, looking
toward Henry at the front of the church — a slightly theatrical angle that places the
viewer among the audience witnessing the speech.

HISTORICAL ACCURACY NOTES:
- No authentic transcript of the speech exists — it was reconstructed decades later by
  William Wirt from the memories of eyewitnesses (principally St. George Tucker) —
  the famous final line may not be the exact words Henry used
- The phrase "Liberty or Death" resonated partly because it was borrowed from a popular
  Scottish ballad — Henry may have been quoting something his audience would recognize
- The church still stands (St. John's Church is a historic site in Richmond today)
