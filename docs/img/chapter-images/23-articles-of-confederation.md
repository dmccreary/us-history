LEFT COLUMN — "WHAT THE ARTICLES DID" (white text):
A simple bulleted list:
- Created the United States of America as a name and concept
- Gave Congress power to declare war, make treaties, coin money
- Successfully negotiated the Treaty of Paris (1783) ending the Revolution
- Passed the Land Ordinance (1785) and Northwest Ordinance (1787) — governing

RIGHT COLUMN — "CRITICAL WEAKNESSES" (amber text, suggesting caution):
- Congress could NOT tax — could only request money from states; states regularly
  refused; the national government was perpetually broke
- Congress could NOT regulate interstate or foreign commerce — states taxed each
  other's goods; economic chaos resulted
- No executive branch — no one to enforce laws or coordinate policy
- No national judiciary — no way to resolve interstate disputes
- Each state had ONE vote regardless of population — large states furious
- Laws required 9 of 13 states to pass; amendments required unanimous agreement —
  nearly impossible to get anything done
- States issued their own currencies; inflation was rampant; debts from the Revolution
  unpaid
